Chroma / Kroma

2022

My Turkish translation of Derek Jarman’s Chroma / Kroma is an unpublished artist’s copy. The volume is bound in a handpicked holographic cardboard cover sourced from the remaining stock of an old stationery shop and otherwise follows the original text.
